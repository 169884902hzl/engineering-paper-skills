# Incomplete Related Work Notes Prompt

Use $engineering-writing to draft a bounded Related Work positioning paragraph.

Rough prior-work notes:

- fixed camera manipulation: useful when object pose stays visible
- active view selection: often for coverage, inspection, or grasp planning
- tactile or force guarded insertion: catches contact problems during execution
- closest paper might combine perception and compliant insertion, citation not
  verified yet

Current paper:
Confidence-triggered RGB-D view acquisition for insertion-pose refinement before
guarded execution.

Boundary:
Use citation placeholders. Do not claim novelty. Do not say prior work lacks a
feature unless the assumption is in the notes. Put citation verification needs
after the draft, not inside the manuscript paragraph.
