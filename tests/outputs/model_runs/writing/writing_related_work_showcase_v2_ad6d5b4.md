Contact-rich insertion exposes a coupling between visual ambiguity and guarded motion that is only partly covered by common routes in prior work. Fixed-view insertion systems can support repeatable manipulation when the sensing geometry is controlled, but the view is typically selected before contact changes visibility or occlusion patterns [FixedViewInsertion]. Active perception methods acquire additional observations for manipulation or inspection, yet the supplied positioning ties those views primarily to object coverage or detection confidence rather than to insertion-pose refinement that is handed to a guarded executor [ActivePerceptionManipulation]. Conversely, force-guarded insertion controllers monitor contact during execution and can stop or slow unsafe motion, but they operate after the visual pose estimate has already been committed [ForceGuardedInsertion]. The closest neighboring line combines perception with guarded insertion, but the available evidence does not establish the same closed pipeline in which low confidence triggers a new view, the insertion pose is refined from that view, and the resulting command is executed under force and pose guards [PerceptionGuardedInsertion]. We therefore position the proposed system at this interface: a confidence-triggered sensing, pose-refinement, and guarded-execution pipeline for contact-rich insertion, without claiming that any individual component is novel in isolation.
