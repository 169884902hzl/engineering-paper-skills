The full system reached 84% success over 180 contact-rich peg insertion trials under partial visual occlusion, outperforming fixed overhead sensing at 69%, fixed side sensing at 72%, and open-loop execution at 61%. The view-specific comparisons show complementary failure modes: the overhead view tends to lose the hole near final approach, while the side view preserves lateral visibility but leaves depth uncertain. Open-loop execution performs worst because pose error is carried into contact rather than corrected during the task.
