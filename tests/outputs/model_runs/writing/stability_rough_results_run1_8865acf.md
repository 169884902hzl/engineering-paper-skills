In the evaluated tabletop setup, the full system achieved the highest success rate, reaching 84% over 180 trials, compared with 69% for the overhead-fixed variant, 72% for the side-fixed variant, and 61% for open-loop execution. The ranking reflects where each reduced configuration loses task-critical information: the overhead-fixed view tends to lose the hole near the end of the motion, the side-fixed view preserves the lateral relation but leaves depth less reliable, and open-loop execution carries residual pose error into contact. These results suggest that reliability comes from updating the target pose before contact rather than relying on a fixed viewpoint or fixed execution plan. The comparison is descriptive and limited to one tabletop setup and one cylindrical peg family; cross-robot deployment remains outside this evaluation.
