The full system achieves the highest success rate, reaching 84% over 180 tabletop insertion trials, compared with 72% for the fixed side-view variant, 69% for the fixed overhead variant, and 61% for open-loop execution. The ranking reflects where pose information is lost during the task rather than only the number of camera views: the overhead view can lose the hole near the end of the motion, while the side view preserves the sideways relation but leaves depth less reliable. Open-loop execution performs worst because pose error is carried into contact instead of being corrected before execution. We report these rates as a descriptive comparison on one tabletop setup and one cylindrical peg family; cross-robot behavior, deployment performance, and statistical significance are outside this evaluation.
