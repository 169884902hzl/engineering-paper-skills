Peg-hole insertion becomes hardest near contact, when the peg itself occludes the hole and early pose errors can be carried directly into contact execution. Our system combines overhead and side RGB-D views to estimate both insertion pose and confidence: the overhead view provides an initial global view, while the side view helps preserve lateral information when the hole becomes less visible. When confidence is low, the system re-observes the scene and updates the pose estimate before executing guarded insertion with force and pose-deviation stop conditions. Across 180 trials, the full system reaches 84% success, compared with 69% for overhead-only sensing, 72% for side-only sensing, and 61% for open-loop execution. The ranking indicates that multi-view confidence-aware pose updating, rather than either single view alone, is the main source of improvement in this evaluated setup. These results are bounded to one 7-DoF arm, one fixture, and one cylindrical peg family; statistical significance, cross-robot transfer, and deployment performance remain outside the present evaluation.
