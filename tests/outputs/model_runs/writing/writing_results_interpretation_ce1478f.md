In the tested tabletop occlusion setup, the full system achieved 84% insertion success across 180 trials, compared with 69% for the fixed overhead camera, 72% for the fixed side camera, and 61% for the open-loop controller. This result indicates that the complete configuration performed better than both fixed-view variants and the open-loop baseline in this single-arm, single-fixture, single-object-family evaluation. Because the experiment does not include confidence intervals, significance tests, cross-robot trials, industrial deployment tests, or an ablation separating view selection from guarded execution, the result should be interpreted as setup-specific evidence of improved insertion success rather than as proof of statistical significance, causal isolation, broad robustness, or deployment readiness.
