Across the reported trials, the full system ranks first, reaching 73% retrieval success over 150 trials compared with 52% for tray-level proposal alone and 64% when wrist-camera verification is added. The 12 percentage point gain from proposal-only to proposal-plus-verification indicates that rechecking the candidate at the wrist camera filters some proposal-stage errors, which is consistent with reflective tool handles confusing tray-level object proposals. The remaining gap between 64% and the 73% full system shows why verification is not sufficient by itself: thin tools can still pass the visual check but fail during the subsequent lift, where the guarded lift check provides an additional execution-stage test before placement in the return bin. These results support the role of staged proposal, verification, and lift checking in the evaluated workbench retrieval setting; the evidence is limited to two tray layouts and six tool categories, with no significance test, factory deployment, or multi-robot evaluation.
