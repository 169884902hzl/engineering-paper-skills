## Draft

Active-observer insertion remains challenging when the target becomes difficult to see during execution. The proposed observer camera keeps the target and gripper visible during the evaluated insertion task, providing the perception signal used by the controller. Across 120 real trials, the method achieved 86% success compared with 71% for the baseline. This result supports a bounded claim that active observation improves insertion success under the evaluated visibility condition. The current evidence does not support complete target invisibility, because the supplied limitation states that the method fails under that condition.
