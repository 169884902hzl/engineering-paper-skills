## Draft

Robotic inspection under partial occlusion remains challenging when fixed cameras miss defects as the part rotates away from the main view. The proposed system addresses this bounded setting by requesting a secondary view when confidence is low, allowing the inspection process to recover task-relevant visibility before final localization. On the supplied one-fixture evaluation, the system reached 92% defect localization accuracy, compared with 81% for the fixed-camera baseline. This supports a local claim that confidence-triggered secondary viewing improves defect localization in the tested fixture and object family, while deployment performance and statistical significance remain unverified.

## Evidence boundary

- Used: partial-occlusion inspection task, secondary-view request, 92% result, 81% baseline, one inspection fixture.
- Not supplied: citations, additional baselines, deployment evaluation, statistical significance test.

## Do-not-claim

- Do not claim deployment readiness.
- Do not claim significance without a statistical test.
- Do not generalize beyond the tested fixture and object family.
