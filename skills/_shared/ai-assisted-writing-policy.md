# AI-Assisted Writing Policy

Use this when drafting, polishing, auditing, responding, or validating text that
may be shaped by AI tools. The author remains responsible for every factual
claim, citation, experiment statement, and final wording.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not invent citations, data, experimental outcomes, mechanisms, reviewer
  actions, line numbers, or venue compliance statements.
- Do not let AI-generated prose make a claim stronger than the source material.
- Do not conceal missing evidence behind fluent language.
- Do not present AI output as verified manuscript truth.
- If the venue requires AI-tool disclosure, flag the disclosure as a validation
  item rather than writing a false compliance statement.

## Disclosure And Responsibility Check

```text
AI writing check
| Text or workflow item | AI-assisted risk | Author verification needed | Venue disclosure status | Action |
```

Use `NOT_VERIFIED` when the user has not provided venue policy, source evidence,
or author confirmation.

## High-Risk AI-Smell Patterns

| Pattern | Why risky | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| empty importance | hides absent motivation | name the concrete task failure |
| adjective stack | replaces evidence with tone | map each adjective to metric or delete |
| mechanical connector | simulates flow | state the real logical relation |
| vague subject | hides actor or mechanism | name the system, method, result, or limitation |
| fluent unsupported claim | sounds plausible without proof | downgrade or mark evidence needed |
