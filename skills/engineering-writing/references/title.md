# Title

Use this for title drafting or title audits.

## Job

The title should tell the reader:

- the task or object
- the core capability or formulation
- the critical condition or application boundary

## Pattern

```text
[Capability/Formulation] for [Task/Object] under [Critical Condition]
```

or:

```text
[System/Method] Enables [Capability] in [Setting]
```

## Avoid

- slogan titles
- grant-objective titles
- unsupported adjectives such as `robust`, `general`, `efficient`, `adaptive`
  unless the evidence defines the regime
- adding every module name
- titles that promise broader scope than the experiments

## Title Audit

Ask:

1. Does the title match the paper's strongest supported contribution?
2. Does it avoid unsupported novelty or generality?
3. Does every important title term appear consistently in Abstract,
   Introduction, Methods, and Results?
4. Would a reader know the task before seeing the method?
