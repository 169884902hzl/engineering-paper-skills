# Style Guardrails

Use this for sentence-level English polish.

## Academic Engineering Style

- Prefer concrete subjects: `the observer arm`, `the controller`, `the ablation`.
- Prefer active verbs when the actor matters.
- Use passive voice when the procedure or result matters more than the actor.
- Keep long sentences for conditions and relations, not filler.
- Avoid excessive nominalization when a verb is clearer.
- Keep terminology consistent across title, abstract, figures, and sections.

## Transitions

Use transitions to state relation, not decoration:

- contrast: `However`, `In contrast`, `Unlike`
- evidence: `Consistent with this`, `This pattern indicates`
- limitation: `However`, `This result is limited to`
- consequence: `As a result`, `This allows`

Avoid using transitions to force unrelated sentences together.

## Punctuation

- Avoid em dashes by default.
- Use parentheses for short clarifications.
- Use colons sparingly for definitions or lists.
- Keep equations and variable references exact.
