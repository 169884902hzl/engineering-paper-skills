# Paragraph Function

Use this before polishing a paragraph.

## Choose One Primary Job

| Job | Paragraph should answer |
|---|---|
| Background | What need, metric, constraint, or capability gap motivates this? |
| Prior work | What class of methods exists, where does it apply, and what remains missing? |
| Problem | What limitation is being isolated and why does it matter here? |
| Method | What object, variable, mechanism, flow, constraint, or validation route is described? |
| Experiment | What was measured, compared, changed, and what does it support? |
| Section opening | What previous basis, section problem, method path, and validation plan are introduced? |
| Section summary | What was completed, what problem was addressed, and how does it support the next step? |
| Outlook | What boundary remains and what direction follows from it? |

If a paragraph tries to do two jobs, split it or make one job subordinate.

## Function-First Rewrite

Before sentence polish, write:

```text
Paragraph job:
Known facts:
Evidence:
Boundary:
First sentence role:
Final sentence role:
```

Then rewrite.

## Common Misdiagnosis

- A sentence may look awkward because the paragraph job is wrong.
- A paragraph may look verbose because it is doing setup, method, and result at
  once.
- A strong final sentence may be impossible because evidence is missing.
