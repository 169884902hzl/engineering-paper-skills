# Source-Note Expansion Check

Use this when rough notes, Chinese notes, lab notes, or bullet lists become
English manuscript prose.

```text
Source expansion check
| Source note | Draft sentence | Added information? | Fact / assumption / unsupported | Action |
```

Do not let the rewrite add:

- experiment numbers
- baselines
- datasets
- citations
- mechanisms
- robustness or generalization claims
- limitations not present in the source

If a sentence is needed for readability but not supplied by the source, mark it
as a placeholder or assumption rather than final manuscript prose.
