# Whole-Paper Style Drift

Use this when multiple sections have been polished separately.

## Audit

```text
Style drift audit
| Term / claim / cadence | Locations | Drift type | Risk | Repair |
```

## Drift Types

- same method renamed across sections
- claim strength grows from Results to Conclusion
- repeated transition patterns create AI-like cadence
- section tone shifts from technical to promotional
- limitations disappear after Discussion

Prefer consistent terms and concrete subjects over varied wording for technical
objects.
