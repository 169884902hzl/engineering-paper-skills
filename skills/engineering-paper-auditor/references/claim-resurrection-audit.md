# Claim Resurrection Audit

Use this when Abstract, Discussion, Conclusion, or response text may revive a
claim that the body of the manuscript did not support.

## Audit

```text
Claim resurrection audit
| Claim | Location revived | Strongest body evidence | Missing proof | Safe wording | Action |
```

## High-Risk Locations

- Abstract final sentence
- Introduction contribution list
- Discussion implication paragraph
- Conclusion first and last sentence
- Reviewer response summary

If the strongest body evidence is absent, the action is `delete` or
`downgrade`, not polish.
