# AI Disclosure Check

Use this before claiming venue readiness when AI tools may have assisted
writing, citation work, figures, analysis, or response drafting.

```text
AI disclosure check
| Workflow item | AI assistance possible? | Author verified? | Venue policy checked? | Status | Action |
```

If the venue policy was not inspected, do not say the paper is compliant. Mark
the check `NOT_RUN` or `PARTIAL`.
