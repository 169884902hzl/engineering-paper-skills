Conservative Claim Audit

Input:
Use $engineering-paper-coach to audit this claim.

Evidence:
- 84% success over 180 trials.
- One robot arm.
- One tabletop fixture.
- One object family.
- No cross-robot or deployment test.

Draft:
"The system is deployment-ready and proven robust
for industrial insertion."
Output:
Diagnosis: NOT_READY as written.

Blocking issues:
- "deployment-ready" is unsupported.
- "proven robust" exceeds one-setup evidence.
- "industrial insertion" is not tested.

Safe rewrite:
"In the evaluated tabletop insertion setup, the
system achieved 84% success over 180 trials on
one robot arm and one object family. Broader
industrial deployment remains untested."

Research Notes To Paper Skeleton

Input:
Use $engineering-paper-coach to turn these notes
into a conservative paper skeleton.

Task:
Contact-rich insertion with visual occlusion.

Method:
Multi-view RGB-D observation requests a new view
when confidence is low; guarded execution stops
under force or pose-deviation limits.

Evidence:
84% success over 180 trials.
Baselines: fixed overhead 69%, fixed side 72%,
open-loop 61%.

Boundary:
One robot, one tabletop fixture, one object family.
No industrial deployment or significance test.
Output:
Story skeleton:
1. Problem: fixed views can lose visibility during
   contact-rich insertion.
2. Gap: fixed-view and open-loop variants
   underperform in the tested tabletop setup.
3. Method: a confidence-triggered multi-view policy
   with guarded execution.
4. Evidence: 84% success over 180 trials, compared
   with 69%, 72%, and 61% baselines.
5. Boundary: one robot, one fixture, one object
   family, and no statistical test.

Safe contribution wording:
"We evaluate a perception-guided guarded insertion
system in a tabletop occlusion setting."

Skeleton To Bounded Paragraph

Input:
Use $engineering-writing to draft a Results paragraph.
Do not add experiments, baselines, statistics,
objects, venues, or citations.

Experiment question:
Does the proposed multi-view guarded insertion
system improve success over fixed-view and
open-loop variants?

Results:
Full system: 84% over 180 trials.
Fixed overhead: 69%.
Fixed side: 72%.
Open-loop: 61%.

Boundary:
One robot, one fixture, one object family.
No statistical significance test.
Output:
The full system achieved 84% success over 180
trials in the evaluated tabletop occlusion setup,
compared with 69% for the fixed overhead camera,
72% for the fixed side camera, and 61% for the
open-loop controller. These results support a
bounded comparison under the tested protocol:
combining view selection with guarded execution
improves success relative to the supplied
fixed-view and open-loop variants in this setup.
The experiment does not establish cross-robot
robustness, industrial deployment readiness, or
generalization beyond the tested object family and
fixture geometry.

Conservative Polishing

Input:
Use $engineering-polishing to polish this paragraph.
Preserve all facts and do not strengthen the claim.

Draft:
"Our method is a robust and general solution for
insertion. It works well in realistic settings and
shows strong performance against baselines. This
demonstrates that active observation is an effective
mechanism for industrial manipulation."

Evidence:
84% over 180 trials; one robot; one fixture;
one object family; fixed-view and open-loop
baselines; no industrial or causal ablation test.
Output:
Polished version:
"In the evaluated tabletop insertion setup, the
proposed system achieved 84% success over 180
trials, compared with the supplied fixed-view and
open-loop baselines. This result supports a bounded
performance improvement under the tested protocol.
The current evidence does not establish industrial
readiness, broad generalization, or a causal
mechanism for active observation."

Rejected wording:
- robust and general solution
- realistic settings
- effective mechanism
- industrial manipulation

Figure/Table Claim Check

Input:
Use $engineering-figure-table to audit this caption.

Figure 2:
Workflow diagram only.

Table 1:
Full system: 84%.
Fixed overhead camera: 69%.
Fixed side camera: 72%.
Open-loop controller: 61%.

Draft caption:
"Figure 2 validates robust active observation and
proves industrial suitability."
Output:
Verdict: FAIL for caption claim strength.

Supported:
- Figure 2 can illustrate workflow states.
- Table 1 can support a bounded comparison in
  the tested tabletop protocol.

Unsupported:
- Figure 2 does not validate robustness.
- The supplied data do not prove industrial use.

Safe caption:
"Figure 2 illustrates the perception, view-request,
guarded-execution, and stop states. Quantitative
success rates are reported in Table 1."

Reviewer Response Truthfulness

Input:
Use $engineering-response to audit this response.

Reviewer:
"The paper overclaims robustness and has no
stress-test evidence."

Supplied material:
No revised manuscript diff.
No source data for a new stress-test table.
Final line numbers are unavailable.

Draft response:
"We added Table 3, new stress tests, and final
line numbers."
Output:
Verdict: FAIL.

Unsupported response claims:
- Table 3 was not supplied.
- New stress-test data were not supplied.
- Final line numbers are not available.

Safe response plan:
"We will narrow the robustness claim to the
evaluated tabletop protocol and mark the planned
stress-test table as not yet completed. Final line
numbers will be added after the compiled revision
is available."

Validation Should Not Say Ready

Input:
Use $engineering-validation to triage readiness.

Available:
- Draft PDF was read once.
- No LaTeX build was run.
- No citation metadata check was run.
- No figure source-data check was run.
- No official venue policy was checked.
- No final line numbers.
- No response diff.

User request:
"Can I say the paper is ready?"
Output:
Readiness verdict: NOT_READY / CANNOT_DETERMINE.

Check states:
- LaTeX build: NOT_RUN
- Citation metadata: NOT_RUN
- Figure/table source data: NOT_RUN
- Official venue policy: NOT_RUN
- Response diff: NOT_RUN
- Final line numbers: NOT_RUN

Allowed statement:
"The draft has received a dry-read triage, but
required submission checks have not been run."

Forbidden statement:
"The paper is submission-ready."

Mini Walkthrough

A compact workflow shows how rough evidence becomes safer manuscript language.

Rough evidence:
Method: multi-view RGB-D insertion with guarded execution.
Results: 84% over 180 trials.
Baselines: fixed overhead 69%, fixed side 72%, open-loop 61%.
Boundary: one robot, one fixture, one object family.
Unsafe draft:
"Our method provides a robust and deployment-ready solution for industrial insertion."
Coach audit:
Verdict: FAIL.

Why:
- "robust" is not supported by one setup.
- "deployment-ready" is not supported by tabletop-only evidence.
- "industrial insertion" is not tested.
Safe manuscript sentence:
"In the evaluated tabletop setup, the system achieved 84% success over 180
trials and improved over the supplied fixed-view and open-loop baselines.
Broader industrial deployment and cross-platform robustness remain untested."
