# Abstract Thin Evidence

## Before

```text
We propose a robust robotic insertion framework that generalizes to cluttered
scenes and outperforms prior methods. Our active camera improves reasoning and
achieves strong performance.
```

## Problem

The draft claims robustness, generalization, prior-method comparison, reasoning,
and strong performance without giving numbers, baselines, tasks, or boundaries.

## After

```text
This work studies robotic insertion under partial visual occlusion. We use an
active observer camera to keep the target and gripper visible during guarded
execution. The available notes describe the method but do not yet provide
validated baselines, quantitative results, or stress-test evidence. As a
result, the current abstract should be treated as a scaffold rather than a final
claim-bearing summary.
```

## Skill Behavior

- Use `engineering-writing`.
- Produce scaffold when method/evidence/boundary are thin.
- Do not invent baselines, citations, numbers, or robustness claims.
