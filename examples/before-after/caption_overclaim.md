# Caption Overclaim

## Before

```text
Fig. 5 proves that the controller is robust to calibration error and generalizes
to cluttered insertion scenes.
```

## Problem

The figure only shows a workflow montage. It cannot prove robustness,
calibration tolerance, or generalization.

## After

```text
Fig. 5 illustrates the execution workflow used in the insertion trials,
including target detection, visual alignment, and guarded insertion. Robustness
and generalization claims require separate experimental evidence.
```

## Skill Behavior

- Use `engineering-figure-table`.
- State visible content only.
- Move unsupported robustness claims to Experiments or mark them unsupported.
